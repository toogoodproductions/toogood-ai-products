Four machines that make video without a shoot.

toogood builds production systems, not one-off films. Each of these four takes a single setup and runs it again and again, so the second video costs a fraction of the first and the hundredth costs almost nothing. Everything below is real work. Press play on any of it.

Product 01

Founder Branding Autopilot

An AI clone of the founder plus a video editing agent, wired together into a machine that puts out a video a day on real topics from the founder's own industry.

What it is for
Continuous presence. Mass production of talking head video so a founder shows up every day without booking a single hour of camera time.
What it is not
Not creative viral video chasing views. That is a different job with a different price. This is volume and consistency.
Input from client
One clone session. After that, topics only.
Output
Talking head videos, daily, in the founder's face and voice.
Product 02

Video Editing Automation

Long form goes in, short form comes out. An agent that cuts a podcast or an interview into reels without an editor sitting through the footage.

Scope
Talking head footage only. This is deliberate. Narrow input is what makes the automation reliable.
Input from client
The long form file. A podcast episode, a webinar, a recorded interview.
Output
Cut reels, captioned and graded, ready to post.
Who it suits
Anyone already recording long form and not getting short form out of it.
Product 03

Repeat Video Automation

Build the format once, then run it for as long as the client needs it. This is the widest of the four, because most video a company needs is the same video again with different content in it.

Training and SOP
Process videos for L&D departments, factories and floor staff. One module built, the rest of the series follows the same spine.
Explainers
Any industry. The format holds, the subject changes.
Owned characters
A mascot or presenter a company owns outright and can use forever, without a fee per appearance.
Channels
Faceless YouTube channels and Instagram reels that need output every week, not once.
Product 04

Static Ads Engine

Carousels, posts and static ads at volume. Point it at a blog and it returns the whole carousel. Or hand it one reference image and it rebuilds that look around your business.

From a blog
One article in, a finished multi slide carousel out.
From a reference
Give it any post whose style you want to borrow. It converts the trend into your product, your language, your market.
From nothing
It can also work the industry on its own and come back with the idea.
Formats
Instagram posts, carousels, static ads, and video carousels.
Scale of the problem
Kotak Mahindra Bank's static creative operation was described on the ThinkSchool podcast. Large brands run creative at a volume no manual design team can match.
